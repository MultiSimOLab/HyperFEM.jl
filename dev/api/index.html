Backends

HyperFEM.Backends — Module

Assembly backends of HyperFEM: who evaluates and assembles the weak forms in the drivers and who stores and updates the state variables. LazyBackend (:lazy, the default) is Gridap's lazy cell-by-cell evaluation; CompiledBackend (:compiled) the compiled, threaded kernels of HyperFEMKernels, enabled by its extension (using HyperFEMKernels). Every backend keyword accepts the symbol or the backend itself (as_backend).

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.AdaptiveStepping — Type
AdaptiveStepping(; Δt, t_end, t0=0.0, rtol=1e-3, atol=1e-6, safety=0.9, fmin=0.2, fmax=2.0,
                 Δtmin=1e-8 (t_end - t0), Δtmax=t_end - t0, controller=:PI, maxreject=20,
                 maxbisec=15, tstops=())

Control of the time loop of a transient model by the local error estimate of its scheme. Δt is the first increment. After a converged attempt the weighted error err of the step (the RMS over the degrees of freedom with a time derivative of the estimate divided by atol + rtol max(|x|, |xₙ|)) decides: the step is accepted when err ≤ 1 and rejected otherwise (retried with the new increment; maxreject consecutive rejections stop the run). The next increment is Δt · f with the factor of the controller, :I (f = safety err^(-1/(p+1)), p the order of the scheme) or :PI (f = safety err^(-kI) (err_prev/err)^(kP), kI = 0.3/(p+1), kP = 0.4/(p+1), Gustafsson; the :I factor on the first step and after a rejection), f clipped to [fmin, fmax], the increment to [Δtmin, Δtmax]; the increment is never grown right after a rejection. A failed attempt (the stage solve did not converge) is bisected as in TimeStepping, without consulting the controller; maxbisec consecutive bisections stop the run. tstops are reached exactly.

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.BDF — Type
BDF(k)

Backward differentiation formula of order k = 1 (backward Euler) or 2, with variable step (ω = Δtₙ/Δtₙ₋₁): ∂t u = c₀ uₙ₊₁ − h, c₀ = (1+2ω)/((1+ω)Δt), h = ((1+ω) uₙ − ω²/(1+ω) uₙ₋₁)/Δt (k = 2, started with k = 1); c₀ = 1/Δt, h = uₙ/Δt (k = 1). The field is evaluated at uₙ₊₁. Error estimate by Milne's device against the explicit predictor of the same order.

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.CentralDifference — Type
CentralDifference()

Explicit central differences for second-order problems: the stage unknown is the acceleration aₙ₊₁, the field is known, uₙ₊₁ = uₙ + Δt vₙ + ½Δt² aₙ (field(ctx, u), α₀ = 0), the velocity vₙ₊₁ = vₙ + ½Δt(aₙ + aₙ₊₁) (∂t(ctx, u), implicit in the acceleration only through a damping term) and ∂tt(ctx, u) = aₙ₊₁. The stage solve is the mass system (mass_lumping = true for a diagonal one); the stability limit (CFL) is the user's. Order 2; no error estimate.

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.GeneralizedAlpha — Type
GeneralizedAlpha(ρ∞)
GeneralizedAlpha(; αm, αf, β, γ)
Newmark(; β=1/4, γ=1/2);  HHT(α);  Midpoint()

Chung–Hulbert family for second-order problems. Newmark relations aₙ₊₁ = (uₙ₊₁ − ũ)/(βΔt²), ũ = uₙ + Δt vₙ + Δt²(½−β) aₙ, vₙ₊₁ = vₙ + Δt((1−γ) aₙ + γ aₙ₊₁); the residual takes the acceleration (1−αm) aₙ₊₁ + αm aₙ (∂tt(ctx, u)), the velocity (1−αf) vₙ₊₁ + αf vₙ (∂t(ctx, u)) and the field (1−αf) uₙ₊₁ + αf uₙ (field(ctx, u)) at t_{n+1−αf}. ρ∞ ∈ [0, 1] gives αm = (2ρ∞−1)/(ρ∞+1), αf = ρ∞/(ρ∞+1), γ = ½ − αm + αf, β = ¼(1 − αm + αf)². Newmark is αm = αf = 0; HHT(α) is αm = 0, αf = α, γ = ½ + α, β = ¼(1+α)²; Midpoint() is ρ∞ = 1 (αm = αf = ½, β = ¼, γ = ½: uₙ₊₁ − uₙ = Δt (vₙ + vₙ₊₁)/2 and the forces at u_{n+½}). Order 2. Error estimate of Zienkiewicz–Xie extended to the family, C Δt²(aₙ₊₁ − aₙ) with C = β − (1−αm)/(6(1−αf)) (β − 1/6 for Newmark; the schemes with C = 0 have no estimate).

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.LoadStepping — Type
LoadStepping(; nsteps=20, maxbisec=15, grow=1.0, easy=10)

Control of the incremental loop of solve! for a StaticNonlinearModel: the stage parameter Λ runs from 0 to 1 in increments ΔΛ, starting at ΔΛ0 = 1/nsteps (an increment may be cut by the safe load factor of the Dirichlet projection). A failed increment is bisected (ΔΛ/2 from the last converged state); maxbisec is the maximum number of consecutive bisections, the count restarts at every converged increment. After an increment converged in at most easy Newton iterations the next one grows, ΔΛ ← min(grow ΔΛ, ΔΛ0); with grow = 1 (the default) the increment is never grown again after a bisection or a cut. A NamedTuple (nsteps = …, maxbisec = …) is accepted wherever a LoadStepping is.

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.Prescription — Type
Prescription

What is prescribed on a boundary. p(Λ, τ) returns an object accepted by Gridap for a single tag: a Number/VectorValue, a function of the coordinates x -> value, or a CellField.

Two parameters are passed by the drivers:

  • Λ, the load: the parameter of the loading programme of the whole simulation. Separable and NonSeparable prescribe values as functions of it.
  • τ, the stage: the parameter of the solver, the one res(τ) and jac(τ) receive. A Coupling prescribes the state of another problem as a function of it.

They coincide in a static or monolithic solve; in a staggered loop Λ runs over the ramp of the step while τ runs from 0 to 1 inside it (see StaggeredModel). p(Λ) is p(Λ, Λ).

SDIRK(2)

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.StaticNonlinearModel — Type
StaticNonlinearModel(res, jac, U, V, dirichlet; neumann=nothing, nls, xh, xh⁻, backend=:lazy,
                     injectivity=nothing, energy=nothing, tangent_switch=nothing)

res(τ) and jac(τ) are functions of the stage parameter τ of the solver. dirichlet are the BoundaryConditions of U; neumann, if any, the NeumannConditions added to the residual by the driver at the load of every step. The model owns the Dirichlet applicators of its spaces (get_applicators: those of U, ∆U and the space of xh⁻). backend (:lazy, :compiled or an AbstractBackend) selects who evaluates and assembles the forms (see build_operator); assem_U is a custom Gridap assembler for the lazy backend only (see assembler).

injectivity is the measure whose quadrature points must keep det F > 0 (dΩ, or (dΩ, i) when the displacement is the field i of a multi-field space); it gives the injectivity step of InjectivityLS and the safe load factor of the Dirichlet projection (see injectivity_step). energy(τ) = u -> ∫(…)dΩ + … is the total potential energy of the model whose first variation is res(τ): the stored energy of every subdomain (energy(model, km, u, dΩ) of WeakForms) plus the regularisation terms; the driver adds the work of the Neumann loads. It is the energy of the Armijo line search (InjectivityLS(armijo = true)), for minimisation problems only. tangent_switch is the TangentSwitch shared by the HessianRegularization wrappers of the models in jac, which the hybrid Newton (HybridTangent) flips between the consistent and the projected tangent. The solver receives the operator of the backend wrapped in a ModelOperator with these hooks.

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.Theta — Type
Theta(θ)

One-leg θ-method: ∂t u = (uₙ₊₁ − uₙ)/Δt and the field at u_θ = θ uₙ₊₁ + (1−θ) uₙ, time t_θ (field(ctx, u)). θ = 1 is backward Euler, θ = ½ the midpoint rule (order 2), Gridap's ThetaMethod. Error estimate by Milne's device.

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.TimeContext — Type
TimeContext

What the weak forms of a transient model see at a stage: the stage time t, the step Δt, the step count step (the one being computed, n + 1), the stage stage, the coefficients c (StageCoefficients) and the history h (StageHistory: the FE functions uₙ, uₙ₋₁, vₙ, aₙ and the β functions). ctx.u⁻, ctx.u⁻⁻, ctx.v⁻, ctx.a⁻ are the history FE functions; ctx.history the user's history object (history = … of the model), nothing by default. The functions of the contract: field, ∂t, ∂tt, at and their variations δfield, δ∂t, δ∂tt. Indexing ctx[i] gives the context of field i of a multi-field problem: the same scheme, with the β and history functions restricted to that field, for the forms written on the components (∂t(ctx[1], u) with (u, p) the unknowns).

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.TimeStepping — Type
TimeStepping(; Δt, t_end, t0=0.0, maxbisec=15, grow=1.0, easy=10, Δtmax=Δt, tstops=())

Control of the time loop of a transient model with a fixed increment: the time runs from t0 to t_end in increments Δt (the last one cut at t_end, and any of them cut at the next of the exact output times tstops; the proposed increment is kept after a cut). A failed attempt (the stage solve did not converge) is bisected from the last accepted step; maxbisec is the maximum number of consecutive bisections, the count restarts at every accepted step. After a step accepted in at most easy iterations the increment grows, Δt ← min(grow Δt, Δtmax) (Δtmax = Δt by default: back to the initial increment after a bisection); with grow = 1 (the default) a bisected increment is never grown again. The analogue of LoadStepping in time.

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.TransientModel — Type
TransientModel(res, jac, U, V, dirichlet; scheme, mass=nothing, damping=nothing, mass_update=:constant,
               mass_lumping=false, tangent_update=:iteration, physics=nothing, internal=nothing,
               history=nothing, xh, vh=nothing, initial_acceleration=:zero, nls, backend=:lazy,
               neumann=nothing, injectivity=nothing, energy=nothing, tangent_switch=nothing,
               kinetic=nothing, stage_energy=nothing)

Transient driver on the loop of step! with the time scheme (QuasiStatic, BDF, Theta, GeneralizedAlpha and its members, CentralDifference, SDIRK), solved with TimeStepping or AdaptiveStepping by solve!.

Two levels. General: res(ctx), jac(ctx) take the TimeContext of the stage and write the field, its time derivatives and the history with field, ∂t, ∂tt, at (and δfield, δ∂t, δ∂tt, δat on the trial direction). Structured (mass given): res(t), jac(t) are the static forms of StaticNonlinearModel; the model assembles the mass matrix of the bilinear mass form once (mass_update = :constant) or at every attempt (:step), lumps it (mass_lumping), and adds the inertia (and the damping form on the velocity, second order) to the residual and the tangent of the stage; the inner forms are evaluated at the stage field ((1−αf) uₙ₊₁ + αf uₙ for the generalized-α, u_θ for Theta, the known uₙ₊₁ for CentralDifference) and at the stage time, where the neumann loads and the Dirichlet values are taken.

tangent_update: :iteration (Newton with the consistent tangent), :step (linear in the stage unknown with a tangent that depends on the history — assembled once per attempt and solved once from the zero guess, no Newton), :Δt (linear with a tangent that depends on Δt only — assembled and factorised only when Δt changes). Explicit stages use :Δt (the mass alone).

physics receives update_time_step!(physics, Δt) before every attempt; internal(xh, xh⁻) is called when a step is accepted, with the new state and the state of the previous step, after post (the post-processing sees the internal variables the residual of the step was evaluated with); history is a user object available as ctx.history and committed with commit!(history, ctx, xh) when defined. xh is the initial state (and the FE function of the state after every accepted step, over the model's x), vh the initial velocity (second order), initial_acceleration :zero or :solve (M a₀ = −r(0, u₀), structured level, pure mechanics). injectivity, energy and tangent_switch are the hooks of StaticNonlinearModel: energy(t) = u -> ∫(Ψ…)dΩ is the stored energy of the model, reported per step when the solver is verbose (EnergyLog, with the kinetic energy of the mass form — or of the kinetic(vh) functional on the velocity — and the work of the neumann loads) and, on the structured level, turned into the incremental functional of the stage whose gradient is the stage residual (stage_energy) for the Armijo line search and the hybrid tangent; on the general level that functional is the user's stage_energy(ctx) = y -> … on the stage FE function.

HyperFEM.ComputationalModels.component_LInf — Function
component_LInf(u, direction, [Ω])::Float64

Calculate the maximum absolute value of a component of a vector-valued field u. The direction can be a symbol (:x, :y or :z), a component index or a VectorValue. The field is evaluated at the nodes of a first order discontinuous space over Ω, so for higher order fields this is an approximation of the L-inf norm. By default, Ω is the triangulation where the function is defined. It could be useful to find the maximum displacement.

Examples

x_max = component_LInf(uh, :x)
y_max = component_LInf(uh, 2)
d_max = component_LInf(uh, VectorValue(1.0, 1.0, 0.0) / √2)

HyperFEM.Solvers.PETScSolver — Type
PETScSolver(ksp=:cg, pc=:gamg; rtol=1e-6, atol=0.0, maxiter=500, reuse=1, nullspace=nothing,

Linear solver of the assembled tangent through a PETSc KSP (needs using GridapPETSc, which activates the extension). The PETSc binary of GridapPETSc provides everything the solver uses.

  • ksp: Krylov method, :cg (symmetric positive definite tangents, e.g. the projected tangent of HybridTangent), :gmres (general, the consistent tangent near instabilities) or :preonly (apply the preconditioner once: direct solvers).
  • pc: preconditioner, :gamg (PETSc's smoothed-aggregation AMG), :boomeramg (hypre), :mumps (LU of MUMPS, the direct reference), :mumps_cholesky, :lu (PETSc's own LU), :jacobi, :none.

Keep atol = 0 inside a Newton: the right-hand side is the residual, which becomes tiny near convergence, and an absolute tolerance would accept an unsolved increment there.
  • reuse: the preconditioner is rebuilt every reuse numerical setups (lagged AMG: reuse = 3 keeps the hierarchy for three Newton iterations; 1 rebuilds it at every tangent). With a direct solver (:preonly) a lagged factorisation is a modified Newton: the increments solve an old tangent and the quadratic rate is lost, with no failure reported. A new matrix object always rebuilds.
  • nullspace: near-null space of the tangent (free dofs × modes, from near_nullspace), passed to the matrix with MatSetNearNullSpace; the rigid-body modes are essential for AMG on elasticity. GAMG reads it by default; BoomerAMG only with its interpolation-vector options (-pc_hypre_boomeramg_interp_vec_variant). nothing leaves PETSc's default (the constant vector).
  • negate: range of equations multiplied by −1 (tangent rows and right-hand side) before PETSc sees the system; the solution is unchanged. A symmetric saddle-point tangent, concave in the electric or magnetic potential (the monolithic EM system: negate = nu+1:n for the φ block), becomes a positive-real matrix on which GMRES + AMG converge, while AMG on the indefinite form fails.
  • blocksize: block size of the matrix (MatSetBlockSize), e.g. 3 for a single vector field in 3D whose free dofs come in complete nodal blocks (no component-wise Dirichlet masks): GAMG then aggregates nodes instead of scalar dofs, which is what it expects on elasticity. 1 (default) sets nothing.
  • options: extra PETSc options for this solver, e.g. "-pc_gamg_threshold 0.01 -ksp_gmres_restart 50" (the prefix of the solver is added automatically).
  • monitor: print PETSc's residual history (-ksp_monitor).

Three configurations of the plan: PETScSolver(:cg, :gamg; nullspace), PETScSolver(:gmres, :boomeramg; nullspace) and PETScSolver(:preonly, :mumps). The solver reports linear_converged, linear_iterations and linear_info to the Newton: a Krylov solve that does not converge fails the Newton iteration like a failed line search.

The matrix of the Newton cache (CSC) is mirrored once into a CSR matrix that PETSc aliases without copying; at every numerical setup only the values are gathered (threaded), so the conversion costs one pass over the nonzeros and allocates nothing after the first setup. Works with any assembling backend (:lazy, :compiled).

HyperFEM.Solvers.near_nullspace — Method
near_nullspace(U::FESpace) -> Matrix{Float64}

Near-null space of a tangent assembled on the trial space U, as an orthonormal matrix of free dofs × modes: the rigid-body modes (translations and rotations, 3 in 2D and 6 in 3D) of every vector-valued Lagrangian field and the constant mode of every scalar field, interpolated on the space, so that the Dirichlet dofs are left out and the modes of a multi-field space live in their own block (a mode of u is zero on φ). Modes that vanish on the free dofs (e.g. a translation fully constrained by Dirichlet conditions) are dropped; an error if none is left. A vector field with fewer or more components than the space dimension gets its translations only. Independent of the Dirichlet values, so it is computed once per space and passed to the solver: PETScSolver(:cg, :gamg; nullspace = near_nullspace(U)).
